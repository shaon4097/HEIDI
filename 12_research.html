Should I screen B12 in patients on long-term metformin, and how often?
Ask
SourcesMy library (38)BD guidelinesEvidence (guidelines, Cochrane, PubMed)Drug bankMy patients (anonymised)
Answer · 6 citationsgenerated 10:52 · reviewed sources only
Save to libraryMake protocol
Long-term metformin is associated with lower vitamin B12 levels, and the risk rises with dose and duration 12. The ADA recommends periodic measurement of B12 in people treated with metformin, especially those with peripheral neuropathy or anaemia 3. Most guidance does not set a fixed interval; a common practice is yearly testing after 4 years of use or earlier with symptoms 4. In South Asian vegetarian diets baseline intake may already be low, which strengthens the case for testing 5.
From your patients: 214 patients on metformin > 4 years; 37 have no B12 result in 2 years; 9 of them have neuropathy symptoms recorded. Create recall routine
